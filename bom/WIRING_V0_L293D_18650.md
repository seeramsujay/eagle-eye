# Project Eagle-Eye v0 Micro: L293D Dual Coreless Motor + 18650 + FPV Camera Wiring Guide

## 1. Overview & Hardware Architecture
This configuration allows you to build the **Eagle-Eye v0 Micro (300mm Twin-Coreless Flying Wing)** utilizing:
- **Battery:** Single 18650 Li-ion cell (3.7V nominal, 4.2V fully charged, 2500–3000mAh) or 2S 18650 pack.
- **Motor Driver:** **L293D Dual H-Bridge IC** (DIP-16 package).
- **Motors:** Twin 8520 or 716 brushed coreless motors (1 in left wing, 1 in right wing).
- **MCU:** ESP32-WROOM-32 DevKit (powered via 5V boost or directly from 1S/2S into VIN/3V3).
- **FPV System:** FPV Camera + 5.8 GHz VTX (powered from battery rail with LC filter).
- **Ground Link:** LoRa 433 MHz SX1278 or high-speed 2.4 GHz WiFi / ESP-NOW link talking directly to our laptop ground station suite (`main.py`, `video_tracker.py`, `pursuit_controller.py`).

## 2. L293D Parallel-Channel Wiring (Crucial for Coreless Motors)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A single L293D channel is rated for **600 mA continuous (1.2A peak)**. A high-RPM 8520 coreless motor can draw **1.2A–1.8A** under full climb thrust.
> To prevent L293D thermal throttling and reduce internal voltage drop, we wire the internal channels in **parallel**:
> - **Left Motor:** Driven by Channels 1 & 2 tied in parallel ($1.2\text{ A}$ continuous / $2.4\text{ A}$ peak).
> - **Right Motor:** Driven by Channels 3 & 4 tied in parallel ($1.2\text{ A}$ continuous / $2.4\text{ A}$ peak).

### Complete L293D DIP-16 Pinout Interconnection Table

| L293D Pin | Pin Name | Connection | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pin 1** | `1,2EN` | **ESP32 3.3V or 5V Rail** | Enables Left Motor Driver |
| **Pin 2** | `1A` (In 1) | **ESP32 GPIO 13 (PWM Left)** | Connect to Pin 7 |
| **Pin 3** | `1Y` (Out 1) | **Left Motor (-) Terminal** | Tie together with Pin 6 |
| **Pin 4** | `GND` | **Common Ground** | Center heatsinking pin |
| **Pin 5** | `GND` | **Common Ground** | Center heatsinking pin |
| **Pin 6** | `2Y` (Out 2) | **Left Motor (-) Terminal** | Tie together with Pin 3 |
| **Pin 7** | `2A` (In 2) | **ESP32 GPIO 13 (PWM Left)** | Tie together with Pin 2 |
| **Pin 8** | `VCC2` | **18650 Battery (+) [3.7V - 7.4V]**| Motor power supply rail |
| **Pin 9** | `3,4EN` | **ESP32 3.3V or 5V Rail** | Enables Right Motor Driver |
| **Pin 10**| `3A` (In 3) | **ESP32 GPIO 12 (PWM Right)**| Connect to Pin 15 |
| **Pin 11**| `3Y` (Out 3) | **Right Motor (-) Terminal**| Tie together with Pin 14 |
| **Pin 12**| `GND` | **Common Ground** | Center heatsinking pin |
| **Pin 13**| `GND` | **Common Ground** | Center heatsinking pin |
| **Pin 14**| `4Y` (Out 4) | **Right Motor (-) Terminal**| Tie together with Pin 11 |
| **Pin 15**| `4A` (In 4) | **ESP32 GPIO 12 (PWM Right)**| Tie together with Pin 10 |
| **Pin 16**| `VCC1` | **ESP32 5V (or VIN) Pin** | Logic power supply (4.5V - 7V) |

*Note: The **Positive (+) wire** of both the Left and Right coreless motors connects directly to the **18650 Battery (+) rail**.* The L293D switches the negative side to ground via PWM (low-side configuration), or alternatively across the H-bridge.

## 4. Software Stack Integration with Ground Station
Because this micro model uses the exact same protocol and coordinates, you can run the **complete Project Eagle-Eye Ground Station software suite** without changes:
1. Video stream from the nose FPV camera is received via your USB UVC video grabber on the laptop.
2. `video_tracker.py` runs YOLOv8n + ByteTrack on the camera feed to lock onto the target car.
3. `pursuit_controller.py` maps error $\Delta x$ into differential thrust steering commands ($\text{Left} > \text{Right}$ turns right, $\text{Right} > \text{Left}$ turns left).
4. `hud_dashboard.py` displays real-time target locking, artificial horizon, and logs MP4 video.
5. Tap `SPACE` at any time for instant sub-5ms manual FPV override!
